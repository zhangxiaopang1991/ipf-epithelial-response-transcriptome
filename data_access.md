# Data access and provenance

The analysis uses public NCBI GEO accessions GSE10667, GSE24206, and GSE32537, with GPL4133, GPL570, and GPL6244 platform annotations. The project-level accession URLs, access date, and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `docs/data_provenance.md`.

The release should provide a download or verification procedure but should not silently alter the source files. Sample-level identifiers are retained only where needed for reproducibility of the public GEO data and must not be supplemented with inferred donor identities.
